## Required Outcome

`wiredgeese.com` must help Alex Gusev generate cash flow as soon as reasonably possible while building toward a less labor-dependent software business.

The strategy cannot wait for PDE, Alarisa, or another future product to become mature. It also cannot default to an unrelated general freelance practice that consumes the capacity needed to build owned systems. Near-term work should pay now and, where possible, advance the same technical, methodological, product, and portfolio direction.

Commercial success is one of the Wired Geese hypotheses under test. The site must report evidence honestly rather than present economic viability as already established.

## Current Economic Reality

Alex has substantial engineering experience but does not yet have enough large external agentic-AI deliveries to compete primarily through a modern AI client portfolio. Earlier work remains valid evidence of architecture, integration, deployment, operations, maintenance, and long-term responsibility. It must not be represented as extensive prior delivery of modern human-plus-agent systems.

The relevant portfolio must be created through owned work and external commercial experience:

`working system -> external use -> paid use -> repeatable delivery -> customer result -> public evidence -> stronger relevant portfolio`

This makes commercial work both a survival mechanism and a source of the evidence currently missing.

## Aligned Funding Model

The preferred economic loop is:

```text
commercial work
  -> cash
  -> market knowledge
  -> relevant portfolio evidence
  -> reusable capability
  -> stronger TeqFW / ADSM / PDE / Alarisa / related ecosystem
  -> better products and stronger evidence
  -> better commercial opportunities
```

One engagement need not produce every result. The strategic value comes from selecting work that produces several results without compromising the buyer's boundary.

Customer-funded reuse is not automatic. Contract, confidentiality, ownership, licensing, security, and customer-specific requirements may prevent generalization. Reusable learning or capability may strengthen owned work only when Alex has the right and practical ability to reuse it.

## Commercial Work Selection Heuristic

Prefer paid work that creates several of these outcomes:

1. immediate revenue;
2. relevant AI/agent-development portfolio evidence;
3. reusable capability or learning that strengthens TeqFW, ADSM, PDE, Alarisa, Desks, TeqCMS, or related product infrastructure;
4. knowledge about a real payer problem or market.

This is a decision heuristic, not a mathematical gate. Factors such as delivery risk, trust, rights, customer fit, capacity, and strategic distraction still matter.

Work that provides only cash may be accepted for survival. It must be recognized as emergency or non-strategic revenue rather than evidence that the intended business model works. The site must not turn this exception into `hire Alex for anything` positioning.

## Current Commercial Layers

### A. Available Now: Knowledge Products

The currently sellable books are:

- the printed book *Browser as an Operating System for Modern Application Development*;
- the electronic book *Agent-Driven Software Management* / *Управляемая разработка с AI-агентами*, available through Leanpub in Russian and English.

Books may generate direct revenue. Their larger role may be authority, methodology explanation, search acquisition, trust, and a bridge into higher-value ADSM-related work. They are not assumed to provide the main near-term cash flow, and sales or audience claims require evidence.

### B. Paid Early Access And Validation

PDE, Telegram Desk, and Shared Files Desk are working but immature systems or capabilities that may be used in paid pilots and early-access arrangements.

Paid early access exists to discover:

- who values the capability and which problem creates willingness to pay;
- which deployment, trust, and delivery boundaries buyers accept;
- which workflows repeat;
- what remains valuable after initial setup;
- which elements can be standardized;
- whether continuing value and recurring use exist.

`Connect ChatGPT to Telegram` is the current bounded paid-validation path for PDE and Telegram capability. It is not automatically the primary long-term cash engine, a mature SaaS offer, or proof of market demand. Its canonical boundaries are defined under `offers/chatgpt-telegram/`.

Shared Files may enter a paid pilot when a specific payer need and safe scope are agreed. There is not yet enough evidence for a standard Shared Files offer.

### C. Productized Engineering For Near-Term Cash

The primary provisional near-term cash direction is bounded MCP and agent integration work close to the systems Alex is already building:

`existing customer service or API -> controlled MCP access -> authorized AI agent`

Possible bounded components include:

- an MCP server for an existing service or API;
- authentication, authorization, and tool-scope boundaries;
- exposure of selected operations to ChatGPT, Codex, Claude, or another compatible agent environment;
- deployment and configuration;
- logging and operational visibility appropriate to the agreed scope;
- one or more clearly specified agent workflows.

This is not a promise to build arbitrary AI systems. Its value comes from focused integration work around protocols, trust boundaries, deployment, and reusable technology Alex is already developing.

The provisional offer under `offers/mcp-integration/` gives this direction a buyer-understandable boundary while leaving final price, timing, package structure, supported environments, SLA, and enterprise-security claims open. It is the primary commercial hypothesis, not a claim of repeatable delivery already achieved.

### D. ADSM-Related Commercial Work

ADSM may support bounded work on an existing human-plus-agent development process, such as reviewing or improving cognitive context, agent instructions, verification boundaries, repository guidance, or human authority controls.

This remains an emerging offer hypothesis. The buyer problem, deliverable, evidence, repeatability, and commercial scope are not established strongly enough for a canonical offer branch. The ADSM book can support authority and discovery but does not prove service demand.

### E. Future Reusable And Recurring Products

The long-term aim is revenue less directly proportional to Alex's time. A plausible development sequence is:

`bounded engineering -> repeatable integration or pilot -> standardized capability -> independent product -> recurring customer value -> subscription or other recurring revenue`

This sequence is directional, not guaranteed. Repeated work may become a product, remain productized engineering, or end. Subscription is justified only when continuing customer value, continuing use, willingness to pay, support cost, and delivery economics are observed. Recurring revenue is preferred where recurring value exists; it must not be fabricated as an early packaging decision.

## Primary Productized-Engineering Hypothesis

The MCP integration direction deserves an offer-level definition because it satisfies several current requirements:

- it can address a concrete existing customer system rather than depend on future owned-product maturity;
- it uses the same MCP, authorization, integration, and deployment knowledge being developed for PDE and Desks;
- it can create relevant current AI-era delivery evidence;
- repeated integrations may reveal reusable components and payer problems;
- it remains bounded enough to avoid generic AI-consulting positioning.

The offer is provisional because important packaging evidence is missing. External work must reveal which source systems, agent environments, authentication patterns, scopes, deployment models, effort ranges, and outcomes repeat.

## Compounding Reuse

When rights and customer interests permit, the preferred delivery path is:

`bounded customer outcome -> reusable integration pattern -> lower uncertainty or effort in later work -> stronger owned capability -> better offer or product`

Reuse may consist of software, documentation, tests, trust-boundary patterns, deployment knowledge, qualification questions, or rejected assumptions. Reuse must be measured by actual reduced uncertainty, effort, or risk; similarity alone is not proof.

Customer work must not be distorted merely to serve an internal roadmap. The buyer purchases an agreed outcome. Product-direction benefit is a selection advantage for Alex, not permission to reduce the promised result or reuse protected customer material.

## Productization Gates

Before standardizing an offer, price, delivery time, or product, evidence should answer the relevant questions:

- Does a recognizable payer problem recur?
- Can the outcome and exclusions be explained independently of one customer?
- Which parts of delivery actually repeat?
- What effort, risk, and communication recur?
- Which trust and deployment boundaries are accepted?
- Does reuse reduce effort or improve quality across deliveries?
- What support continues after delivery?
- Do customers obtain a measurable or clearly observable result?
- Does value continue after setup?
- Will customers pay for that continuing value?

One paid delivery may justify further testing but not a standard package. Several deliveries without common reuse may still be consulting rather than a product. Recurring use without recurring willingness to pay does not establish a subscription business.

## Acquisition And Conversion

The commercial path is:

`recognizable problem -> bounded outcome -> honest maturity and trust boundary -> relevant evidence -> direct commercial conversation`

The site is the central explanation and evidence surface, not necessarily the only acquisition channel. Referrals, search, technical writing, demonstrations, communities, partnerships, and marketplaces such as Upwork may be tested when they lead with a specific need such as MCP integration, controlled agent access, or a bounded Telegram workflow.

Acquisition profiles must avoid generic `AI consultant`, `JavaScript developer`, or staff-augmentation positioning. Older engineering depth can support trust after the current problem and relevant capability are clear.

Manual qualification, scoping, contracting, payment, and delivery are acceptable while volume is low and boundaries are still being learned. No analytics, CRM, payment, or automated funnel implementation is authorized by this document.

## Portfolio And Evidence Development

Each suitable engagement should produce an internal and, where safe, public record of:

- the payer problem and agreed result;
- actual scope, effort, and trust boundary;
- what was delivered and verified;
- customer feedback or measurable outcome;
- what repeated or was reusable;
- what remained bespoke;
- whether follow-on or recurring value appeared;
- which claim the evidence supports.

`journal-and-evidence.md` governs public chronology and claim boundaries. Confidential work may inform strategy without becoming public proof. The site must narrow public claims when disclosure is unavailable.

## Transferable Development

ADSM-oriented context transfer may become an additional delivery capability when the customer needs another accountable human-and-agent team to continue development. Source access, deployment access, cognitive-context access, rights to modify, support, and ownership remain separate agreement dimensions.

This capability is strategically relevant but is not the immediate cash priority and has not been externally validated. `transferable-development.md` defines its boundary.

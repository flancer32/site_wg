## Site Identity

`wiredgeese.com` is the public and commercial home of Alex Gusev's software-making practice.

It presents an observable model of software creation:

`one accountable human maker + hired AI agents`

Alex chooses direction, defines intent, owns consequential decisions, accepts results, and remains accountable. AI agents are active working participants that help inspect, design, implement, test, document, and operate authorized work. They are not employees, legal actors, owners, or independent commercial decision-makers.

Wired Geese is the brand and public narrative for this model. It is not software, a platform, a company, or a substitute identity for Alex. The name deliberately echoes the Wild Geese: Alex hires capable agents for work in the digital environment. This metaphor is a meaningful brand asset for positioning, demonstrations, and Journal/Event framing, but it must never imply an employment relationship or transfer human accountability to agents.

`F. Lancer`, SIA is Alex's Latvian legal entity for contracts, invoicing, ownership where applicable, and formal commercial relationships. It is not the public brand, development method, or software platform.

## Working Foundation

Tequila Framework (`TeqFW`) is the technical North Star of Alex's work. It is the foundational application platform and primarily answers:

`How is the software structured and built?`

Agent-Driven Software Management (`ADSM`) is the complementary development-management methodology and primarily answers:

`How is software evolution organized and controlled when a human works with AI agents?`

Together, TeqFW and ADSM shape the working practice demonstrated through Wired Geese. They are foundations, not ordinary catalogue products comparable with a Desk, a book, a pilot, an integration, or a SaaS application.

TeqFW has its own primary site at `teqfw.com`. Wiredgeese.com may use technical depth and inspectable TeqFW artifacts as evidence, but it must not become the primary TeqFW marketing or documentation site. Its role is to show what one accountable developer and AI agents can build with the platform and methodology.

ADSM may lead to sellable books, reviews, workflow improvements, or development services. Those commercial objects remain distinct from the methodology itself.

## Own Software As Working Proof

Alex applies the Wired Geese development model to his own current software unless a particular system is explicitly documented otherwise. His own software estate includes more than applications: it also includes current frameworks, libraries and packages, platform and infrastructure components, experimental and product systems, and wiredgeese.com itself.

The working relationship is:

```text
TeqFW + ADSM
       +
Alex as accountable human
       +
AI agents as working participants
       |
       v
Alex's current applications, frameworks, libraries, infrastructure, and systems
       |
       v
working evidence that the Wired Geese development model is practiced
```

AI agents are normal participants in this current development practice. Depending on the authorized work, they may help with analysis, inspection, design, implementation, refactoring, testing, verification, documentation, deployment preparation, or operation. This does not mean every task is performed by an agent, all code is AI-generated, or agents independently own or control development. Alex defines intent, authorizes work, controls consequential decisions, verifies and accepts results, and remains accountable.

wiredgeese.com is recursive evidence: it explains the Wired Geese model while being developed through that same human-directed model. This establishes that the practice is real. It does not by itself establish superior productivity or quality, autonomous development, external usability, market demand, scalability, commercial viability, or economic sustainability.

This principle governs Alex's current development practice. It must not be applied retroactively to historical software without evidence that the agent-supported process was used at the time. `journal-and-evidence.md` defines the claims this internal development evidence can and cannot support.

## Alarisa And Current Systems

Alarisa is the most ambitious current proof system and a long-term product direction built with the TeqFW and ADSM foundation. It explores how a `Principal` and that Principal's Assistant interact in the digital world.

Its strategic relationship is:

`TeqFW + ADSM -> capacity to build increasingly complex systems -> Alarisa as a major proof`

Alarisa is not the North Star above TeqFW and ADSM, not a container into which every project must fit, and not currently a market-ready product. It may eventually become a commercial product or family, but its present value does not depend on that outcome. The proof remains ongoing: the site may document increasing capability, deployment, operation, use, and economic experiments without claiming that Alarisa or the overall model has already been validated.

Personal Digital Embassy (`PDE`) belongs to the Alarisa development direction. It provides controlled digital representation and an MCP entry point through which authorized AI agents can interact with a person's services and resources. PDE, Telegram Desk, and Shared Files Desk are working but early. They can support paid pilots and early-access arrangements, reusable technology, product hypotheses, and evidence; they must not be represented as mature mass-market SaaS.

Other systems do not need to fit inside Alarisa. TeqCMS, for example, may be useful software in its own right, infrastructure for Alex's work, evidence that TeqFW and ADSM work in practice, a source of reusable capability, or a future commercial hypothesis. The product strategy does not impose one deterministic roadmap on all work.

`product-system.md` is authoritative for object kinds, current maturity, and catalogue eligibility.

## Three First-Class Site Jobs

The site must perform three connected jobs.

### Sell

Generate economically useful relationships and near-term cash flow through current books, paid early access, bounded productized engineering, and later reusable products. Visitors must be able to understand what can be bought now, what is experimental, and how to start a relevant commercial conversation.

### Demonstrate

Show real systems and increasingly ambitious work produced through the Wired Geese model and the TeqFW/ADSM foundation. Demonstration includes working software, bounded deployments, technical artifacts, product experiments, and Alarisa's ongoing proof role.

### Document

Maintain a visible chronology of attempts, decisions, implementations, failures, deployments, uses, payments, reuse, changes, and discontinuations. Journal/Events turn activity into cumulative, inspectable evidence rather than promotional assertion.

All three jobs are indispensable, but first-class status does not mean equal short-term priority, visual space, or implementation effort. During the current funding stage, `SELL` has immediate operational priority because aligned cash flow finances the work and creates market contact. `DEMONSTRATE` supplies credible proof that supports selling and the broader Wired Geese thesis. `DOCUMENT` accumulates evidence, trust, acquisition value, and an observable account of engineering and economic progress.

```text
SELL        -> immediate financing and market contact
DEMONSTRATE -> credible proof for selling and the Wired Geese thesis
DOCUMENT    -> cumulative evidence, trust, acquisition, and observability
```

The three jobs reinforce one another. Commercial conversion must not be weakened merely to give them formal symmetry.

The site must not collapse into only a portfolio, catalogue, consultancy site, technical blog, archive, or ideological account of AI-assisted development.

## Economic Proof Hypothesis

Wiredgeese.com makes this question observable:

`Can one experienced, accountable developer working with AI agents through TeqFW and ADSM create useful software and build an economically sustainable software-making practice?`

The answer must emerge from claim-specific evidence rather than marketing assertions. `journal-and-evidence.md` defines the evidence hierarchy, current baseline, and limits on engineering and commercial inferences.

The site therefore documents both engineering progress and the attempt to establish economic viability. Failure, rejection, and discontinuation are valid evidence when described truthfully.

## Current Economic Position

Alex has long engineering experience but not yet a portfolio of large external agentic-AI client projects sufficient to compete mainly through past AI delivery. The relevant new portfolio must be created through current owned work and external commercial delivery. `journal-and-evidence.md` is authoritative for both the value and limits of older work and the progression of current evidence.

Commercial survival cannot wait for Alarisa, PDE, or another future product to mature. The primary near-term funding direction is bounded productized engineering close to the existing technology, especially controlled MCP and agent integrations with real customer services. Paid early access to PDE and Desks is a validation path. Books are already sellable assets and support authority and acquisition, but they are not assumed to be the main cash engine.

The long-term direction is toward reusable products and revenue less proportional to Alex's time. Recurring revenue is desirable only after recurring customer value is observed; subscription is not a default business model.

`commercial-strategy.md` defines this economic model and the current commercial layers. Offer boundaries live under `offers/`.

## Public Communication Boundary

Public communication must be calm, specific, technically credible, commercially clear, and candid about maturity. A first-time visitor should be able to determine:

1. what Wired Geese means and who is accountable;
2. what can be bought now and what is only experimental;
3. what current work demonstrates;
4. how TeqFW and ADSM support the work without becoming the sales object;
5. why Alarisa is ambitious but not yet proven;
6. what relevant next action is available.

Do not use generic `AI expert`, arbitrary freelance, or artificial enterprise positioning. Do not invent urgency, scale, guarantees, customer outcomes, or organizational capacity. Internal terms such as PDE Runtime, Desk architecture, TDLib, or MCP details must not be prerequisites for understanding buyer value.

## Public Surface Principle

Sell, demonstrate, and document are all first-class responsibilities. At the current stage, aligned selling has operational priority; demonstration and documentation remain essential compounding mechanisms rather than optional secondary content. Page responsibility and attention follow the visitor job and current commercial need, not a requirement for equal allocation.

Home must provide immediate comprehension, a commercial path, visible current work, and recent evidence. Commercial destinations must attach evidence to the buyer claim. Journal/Events must be intentionally prominent as the ongoing proof stream. About supplies maker trust. Historical projects, technical articles, books, and other search-bearing material remain discoverable without controlling first-time buyer attention.

`journal-and-evidence.md` defines the evidence stream and claim rules. `information-architecture.md` defines the semantic public hierarchy. `home-page-composition.md` defines Home attention and handoffs.

## Preservation And Multilingual Consistency

`preservation does not imply navigational dominance`

Useful existing articles, books, project pages, technical materials, incoming links, and historical routes must be inventoried and intentionally kept, redirected, or retired during later implementation. Discontinued material must not be promoted as current merely because it remains accessible.

One product meaning governs English, Russian, and Spanish surfaces. Idiomatic copy may differ, but identity, maturity, evidence boundaries, and visitor actions must remain semantically consistent.

This product context authorizes no changes to public copy, routes, UI, source code, or application behavior.

## Purpose

Distinguish the objects shown through Wired Geese so that evidence, products, methods, and commercial work are never confused.

## Canonical Relationship Model

```text
Alex Gusev — accountable human maker
  ├── Wired Geese — brand and public expression of the practice
  ├── engineering system
  │   ├── TeqFW — application platform and technical foundation
  │   └── ADSM — human-directed development-management methodology
  ├── software built with that system
  │   ├── Alarisa — major ongoing proof system and product direction
  │   ├── PDE and Desks — working systems and capabilities
  │   └── TeqCMS, Mindstream, and other systems — independent evidence
  ├── client product builds — the primary commercial capability
  └── books and durable knowledge — separate sellable knowledge objects
```

## Object Boundaries

- **Wired Geese** is Alex's brand, not a software product, agency, or autonomous company.
- **TeqFW** and **ADSM** are components of the engineering system, not a catalogue of customer-selectable services.
- **Alarisa** is the largest current multi-component proof and a long-term product direction. It is not market-ready, completed proof, or a client offer.
- **PDE, Telegram Desk, Shared Files Desk, and TeqCMS** are working systems or capabilities. Working status is not commercial eligibility.
- **Client product build** is an agreed new application or software product created through Alex's system. It may include focused initial work and continued evolution without becoming an hourly-capacity contract.
- **Books** are separate knowledge products with their own availability and purchase facts.

## Evidence Rule

The estate demonstrates ongoing engineering practice and the use of the system across different software types. It does not alone prove market demand, customer satisfaction, delivery speed, external adoption, commercial viability, or a future product outcome. `software-estate.md` and `journal-and-evidence.md` govern those claims.

## Prohibited Reclassification

No current system becomes a commercial offer merely because it exists, uses AI, exposes an integration, or appears in a repository. No document may reintroduce architecture reviews, generic audits, rescue work, generic AI integration, staff augmentation, or the discontinued GitHub Flows / Agent Orchestration PoC as primary commercial objects.

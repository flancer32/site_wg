## Purpose

Define the public hierarchy that explains Alex's engineering system, demonstrates its products and systems, documents its development, and starts suitable new-product conversations.

## Primary Discovery

The persistent shell must make these destinations directly discoverable:

1. **Home** — the proposition: Alex builds products through his own engineering system.
2. **Work with Alex** — fit, ownership boundary, first scope, pricing mechanism, and direct conversation.
3. **Current Work** — Alarisa and smaller systems as evidence, with honest present status.
4. **Journal** — dated evidence, ideas, and development chronology.
5. **How I work** — TeqFW, ADSM, human accountability, and AI-agent participation.
6. **Books and durable knowledge** — separately purchasable knowledge and deeper explanation.
7. **Contact** — a human handoff for a new product or isolated new system.

## Visitor Journeys

The commercial journey is `product need -> approach -> evidence -> fit -> conversation -> engagement`. It is distinct from the knowledge journey `hook -> resonance -> immersion -> reflection/discussion`.

Home leads quickly to the product-building proposition and Work with Alex. Work with Alex explains that clients own the business problem and constraints while Alex owns the engineering approach. Current Work and the Journal substantiate that approach; neither is a product catalogue. Contact asks for product intent, users, capabilities, constraints, and stage—not for architecture diagnosis or framework vocabulary.

## Page Responsibilities

- **Home** establishes the proposition, system, agent role, evidence, and a product-conversation action.
- **Work with Alex** is the commercial destination. It describes suitable product work, scope selection, ownership, price-by-agreed-scope, and non-fit.
- **Current Work and Alarisa** show what has been built and what engineering idea it demonstrates. They do not turn systems into offers.
- **How I work** makes the engineering system understandable without requiring technical depth before a visitor can decide whether the approach fits.
- **Books** retain direct purchase paths, separate from product-development work.
- **Contact** begins a direct, non-automated conversation; it has no form, payment, credential collection, provisioning, or CRM role.

## Legacy And Redirects

The discontinued Agent Orchestration PoC, GitHub Flows, €50 repository pilot, trial/setup funnels, and MCP- or Telegram-led commercial pages are removed from active discovery. Their public routes receive locale-preserving permanent redirects to the closest current destination. Internal links must never depend on those redirects.

## Multilingual Parity

English, Russian, and Spanish retain the same identity, accountability, evidence limits, product-building commercial meaning, and direct contact path. Labels may be idiomatic. Canonical URLs, alternates, locale switching, sitemap inclusion, and redirects must be considered together when a route is retired.

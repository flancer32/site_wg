## Offer Status

`Connect ChatGPT to Telegram` is a bounded paid early-access and commercial-validation offer.

It uses the working but early PDE and Telegram Desk capabilities to establish an agreed Telegram workflow for a customer's MCP-compatible AI environment. `ChatGPT` is the clearest current buyer-facing reference; another compatible agent environment may be used only when it fits the required capability and agreed boundary.

This offer is not mature SaaS, a standard subscription, the assumed primary long-term cash engine, or evidence of validated demand. Its strategic purpose is to deliver real value while discovering who will pay, which outcome matters, which trust boundary is acceptable, what repeats, and whether value continues after setup.

## Buyer Problem And Outcome

The buyer has a Telegram information or publishing workflow that an authorized AI agent could assist with, but lacks a controlled connection between the agent environment and the relevant Telegram account or channels.

The bounded outcome is:

`an agreed AI-to-Telegram workflow operating through an agreed deployment and authority boundary`

Candidate workflows include:

- reading conversations or channels available to the connected account;
- reducing a large message stream to a useful summary;
- retrieving relevant history where the capability supports it;
- preparing or sending messages;
- preparing and publishing channel posts;
- adapting one source item for multiple language-specific channels.

The paid scope must select a specific workflow, account/channel boundary, permissions, AI environment, and acceptance condition. This list is not a promise that every Telegram operation or history mode is supported.

## System And Product Boundary

PDE is the working early system. Telegram Desk is the working early capability. The configured customer workflow is the delivered result. This offer is the commercial presentation of that result.

The buyer does not need to understand or adopt Alarisa, TeqFW, ADSM, PDE internals, Desk architecture, TDLib, or MCP implementation details before understanding the outcome. Those foundations may support trust after the buyer problem is clear.

PDE and Telegram Desk are part of the Alarisa development direction, but purchasing this offer does not purchase Alarisa or validate its larger hypothesis.

## Early-Access Boundary

The current evidence baseline is defined in `../../journal-and-evidence.md`. For this offer, external paid delivery remains a validation objective rather than an achieved repeatability claim.

The offer must remain explicit that delivery may involve manual configuration, discussion, and troubleshooting. It must not imply unattended reliability, generalized support, production-grade security, broad market validation, scalability, or service continuity that has not been established.

## Deployment And Trust Agreement

Telegram integration operates near personal credentials, conversations, channels, and publishing authority. Before delivery, Alex and the customer must agree at least:

- deployment location and administrative control;
- credential ownership and who can access credentials;
- where configuration and secrets are held;
- which account, chats, channels, operations, and permissions are in scope;
- who is responsible for updates and routine operation;
- how access is revoked and the engagement is terminated;
- what evidence may be retained or disclosed.

Possible deployment arrangements may include customer-controlled infrastructure, dedicated infrastructure provisioned for the engagement, or infrastructure controlled by Alex when mutually acceptable. The routinely supported set is unresolved.

The agreement must not promise absolute security, privacy, legal compliance, availability, data recovery, or uninterrupted operation.

## Commercial Boundary

The current commercial form is:

- a paid experimental engagement;
- a fixed initial scope agreed before work begins;
- a price agreed before work begins;
- explicit deployment and credential boundaries before access is requested;
- manual qualification and delivery while the offer is being learned.

No standard public price or delivery time is established. A favorable internal installation taking approximately two hours is planning evidence only, not an external estimate or promise. The discontinued `€50` GitHub experiment is not a price anchor.

Work outside the agreed Telegram result, including bespoke workflows or new integrations, requires separate scoping. Reusable improvements may strengthen PDE only when customer rights, confidentiality, and the agreed boundary permit.

## Validation Questions

Each paid engagement should help answer:

- Which Telegram outcome caused willingness to pay?
- Which buyer profile and acquisition path produced the engagement?
- Which deployment and trust arrangement was accepted?
- What effort and communication were required?
- Which parts repeated or became reusable?
- What failed or remained bespoke?
- Was the workflow used after setup?
- Did continuing or additional value appear?

Results from an engagement must be interpreted under the commercial evidence ladder in `../../journal-and-evidence.md`; they do not change global product or offer status automatically.

## Exclusions

Unless separately agreed, the offer excludes:

- a general-purpose AI automation project;
- arbitrary non-Telegram integrations;
- unlimited workflows, accounts, channels, message volume, hosting, or support;
- transfer of credentials or administrative authority beyond the agreed need;
- hidden bespoke development inside the initial scope;
- source-code or cognitive-context rights;
- legal, privacy, security, availability, compliance, or service-level guarantees.

## Commercial Next Step

The next step is a direct qualification conversation covering the desired Telegram outcome, agent environment, permissions, deployment, trust boundary, initial scope, evidence/disclosure boundary, and likely customization.

Final public copy, price presentation, form fields, routes, payment, provisioning, and delivery procedure remain later decisions.

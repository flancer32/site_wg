## Purpose

Define the attention order and semantic handoffs of the public home page.

## Attention Order

1. Alex builds new web applications and software products.
2. He builds through his own coherent engineering system, not an arbitrary framework selection.
3. TeqFW, modular architecture, contextual documentation, and controlled AI-agent work are parts of that system, developed and revised through practical work rather than offered as a universal method.
4. Alex remains accountable for direction, acceptance, and consequential decisions.
5. Alarisa and smaller current systems provide concrete evidence at different scales; PDE and useful Desks may also be independently usable products.
6. A visitor can either work with Alex on a new product or explore an existing PDE capability.
7. The Journal and durable knowledge provide deeper, dated explanation.

The hero is a thesis, not a list of services. It must lead to `Work with Alex`, with Current Work as the evidence and existing-products handoff.

## Commercial Handoff

Home leads with the new-product proposition but must reveal the separate existing-products path shortly afterward. It may mention focused applications, substantial product development, and ongoing evolution as scales of the first path; it may introduce PDE and Telegram Desk as early usable products without turning the hero into a protocol or services list. It must not lead with MCP integration, AI integration, architecture review, generic consulting, rescue work, or hourly capacity.

The page supplies distinct next steps: a visitor building a new product can start a product conversation, while a visitor seeking an existing PDE capability can explore Current Work or start a PDE conversation.

## Evidence Composition

Alarisa receives prominent space as the major proof of a substantial multi-component system. TeqCMS, PDE, Mindstream, TeqFW, and other systems may demonstrate breadth only where the text makes their current status and evidentiary limits clear. Repository links support inspection; they do not replace explanation or prove customer outcomes.

## Responsive Constraint

On compact viewports, preserve the early proposition, Work with Alex action, and Current Work evidence before secondary biography, technical depth, books, or archive material. The order expresses dependencies, not decorative symmetry.

# Home Page Composition

- Path: `ctx/docs/product/home-page-composition.md`
- Template Version: `20260909`
- Changed: `20260910`

## Purpose

Define Home content responsibilities, attention order, routing semantics, evidence duties, and desktop/mobile constraints for the revised Wired Geese strategy.

This document does not define final marketing copy, exact routes, page components, CSS, media, animation, or application behavior. Layout descriptions are constraints or hypotheses only.

## Home Responsibility

Home must establish, in one coherent visit:

1. what Wired Geese means;
2. who remains accountable;
3. what can create a commercial relationship now;
4. what Alex and the agents are currently building or testing;
5. what recent evidence exists;
6. how TeqFW and ADSM form the foundation;
7. why Alarisa is an ambitious ongoing proof rather than the foundation or a finished product;
8. which systems are working but early;
9. how deeper Journal, system, trust, and historical evidence can be reached;
10. what relevant commercial action a visitor can take.

Home must balance `SELL`, `DEMONSTRATE`, and `DOCUMENT`. It must not read primarily as a product catalogue, consultancy landing page, portfolio, technical manifesto, or blog index.

## Opening Attention Contract

Before significant scrolling, a first-time visitor should be able to understand:

- Wired Geese is Alex Gusev working as one accountable human maker with hired AI agents;
- real work is active and publicly documented;
- at least one concrete commercial path exists now;
- current work, Journal/Events, and the commercial path are directly reachable.

The opening may feature one current commercial path or one current system, but it must not imply that a single Telegram offer, Alarisa, or a product catalogue defines the whole site. TeqFW, ADSM, technical vocabulary, biography, and historical proof must not delay comprehension.

The Wired Geese/Wild Geese metaphor should be understandable in the opening or its immediate continuation. It may shape language and imagery confidently, provided Alex's accountability is explicit and agents are not represented as employees or independent commercial actors.

## Content Sequence

The following order defines attention and semantic dependencies. Adjacent regions may share a visual composition when all responsibilities remain distinct.

### 0. Persistent Header

- **Purpose:** expose the site's stable meanings from every page.
- **Contains:** Wired Geese/Home; commercial entry points; current work; Journal/Events; how the work is made; a distinct commercial action; locale control.
- **Constraint:** Journal must not be hidden only in a footer or archive menu. Exact labels and grouping remain interface decisions.

### 1. Wired Geese Proposition

- **Purpose:** explain the brand, maker, and kind of work immediately.
- **Primary meaning:** Alex builds software systematically with AI agents while retaining direction and accountability.
- **Contains:** compact first-person maker attribution; meaningful Wired Geese metaphor; one practical statement about useful software and digital services; routes to current work and commercial entry points.
- **Excludes:** stack inventory, full TeqFW or ADSM explanation, agent-count claims, career chronology, or corporate `we`.

### 2. Commercial Entry Available Now

- **Purpose:** make near-term economic action concrete.
- **Primary meaning:** bounded MCP integration is the primary provisional engineering direction; Telegram is paid early access/validation; books are available now.
- **Contains:** one leading commercially relevant entry, its true status, recognizable buyer problem, bounded result, and direct next path; concise access to other current layers.
- **Excludes:** a symmetrical card grid that makes books, early access, provisional engineering, and mature products look equivalent; fixed price or delivery promises not supported by evidence.

The leading entry may change as evidence changes. Home architecture must not hard-code Telegram or MCP as a permanent site identity.

### 3. Current Work And Systems

- **Purpose:** demonstrate that the working model produces real and increasingly ambitious systems.
- **Contains:** a selected current view of Alarisa, PDE/Desks, TeqCMS, or other evidenced work; object kind; honest status; one actual capability or active question; route to system detail or related thread.
- **Primary meaning:** working and strategically important do not mean mature or for sale.
- **Excludes:** forced Alarisa hierarchy, raw project inventory, fake roadmap, or automatic conversion of every capability into a product card.

Alarisa must appear as the major proof system and long-term product direction. TeqCMS and other systems must remain representable outside its hierarchy.

### 4. Recent Journal And Events

- **Purpose:** prove that work, learning, and commercial testing are current and observable.
- **Contains:** a small selection of recent meaningful events, including negative or corrective evidence when material; date or sequence context; affected system/offer/hypothesis; the state change or result; route to the event or cumulative thread.
- **Primary meaning:** the Wired Geese hypothesis is being tested in public over time.
- **Excludes:** generic blog teasers, activity theatre, success-only announcements, or old content selected merely for traffic.

Recent activity must have visual and semantic prominence comparable to current work and the commercial entry. It must appear before long foundation, biography, or historical sections.

### 5. Human-Plus-Agents Working Model

- **Purpose:** explain enough of the production model to support trust.
- **Contains:** Alex's responsibility; agents as authorized working participants; how intent, decisions, verification, and boundaries remain human-controlled; route to deeper explanation.
- **Primary meaning:** Wired Geese is a real working arrangement, not an organizational fiction or productivity slogan.
- **Excludes:** claims that agents work autonomously, replace accountability, remove key-person risk, or create large-team capacity.

### 6. TeqFW And ADSM Foundation

- **Purpose:** identify the distinct foundations behind the work without turning Home into platform documentation.
- **Contains:** TeqFW as the technical North Star answering how software is structured; ADSM as the methodology answering how evolution is organized with agents; selected inspectable evidence where useful.
- **Routes:** deeper working-model context, ADSM knowledge or book, and `teqfw.com` for primary TeqFW depth.
- **Excludes:** detailed APIs, architecture, package catalogues, or treating either foundation as an ordinary product tile.

This region may be visually combined with the working-model region if the distinctions remain explicit.

### 7. Alarisa Proof Direction

- **Purpose:** express the most ambitious current proof and long-term product direction.
- **Contains:** the Principal–Assistant ambition; the relationship `foundation -> capacity for complex systems -> Alarisa as proof`; current unproven status; links to relevant systems and Journal threads.
- **Primary meaning:** successful creation, evolution, deployment, and operation of Alarisa would be strong practical evidence, but that proof is still underway.
- **Excludes:** purchase action for Alarisa, completed-proof language, market readiness, or claim that every system belongs to its roadmap.

### 8. Selected Evidence And Accountable Maker

- **Purpose:** reduce remaining uncertainty with claim-matched evidence and maker trust.
- **Contains:** two or three current proofs selected for the claims made; concise Alex identity; relevant older responsibility evidence with its limits; route to About or deeper evidence.
- **Priority:** current working artifact and use; external or paid evidence when it exists; inspectable TeqFW/ADSM/system evidence; older production responsibility.
- **Excludes:** generic evidence wall, CV chronology, testimonials or outcomes that do not exist, or historical work presented as agentic-AI proof.

### 9. Commercial Next Action

- **Purpose:** turn informed interest into a human conversation.
- **Contains:** one dominant action tied to the MCP pilot, Telegram validation, a book, or a closely aligned need; concise expectation setting; originating context where practical.
- **Excludes:** generic invitation to hire Alex for anything, credentials, final scoping form, fixed commercial terms, or claims of automated onboarding.

### 10. Footer And Durable Discovery

- **Purpose:** keep all strategic and retained content families intentionally reachable.
- **Contains:** commercial entry points; current work; Journal/Events; working-model/foundation explanation; About; books; Library/durable knowledge; Projects/history; contact; legal or external identity destinations where approved.
- **Constraint:** the footer is neither a duplicate catalogue nor the sole home of Journal.

## Desktop Composition Constraints

- The opening composition must expose the proposition, current activity, and a commercial path before methodology or biography dominates attention.
- A split opening may pair the proposition with recent work, an event, or the leading commercial entry. No exact column structure is approved.
- Current work, recent Journal/Events, and the commercial entry must each have a recognizable visual role; none may be buried in an undifferentiated card collection.
- Foundation and Alarisa explanations follow visible evidence of real current activity.
- Historical proof and broad archives remain deeper than current work and recent events.
- The page may use editorial asymmetry to reflect what is most current, but status and object kinds must remain clear.

## Mobile Composition Constraints

Mobile is not a mechanical stack of a desktop layout. It must protect the same strategic order with less content.

1. Show the compact Wired Geese proposition and Alex's accountability.
2. Expose direct actions for current work, Journal, and the leading commercial entry.
3. Show the leading commercial result/status without a long preamble.
4. Show one current system or development item.
5. Show recent meaningful events.
6. Explain the human-plus-agents model and TeqFW/ADSM foundation concisely.
7. Present Alarisa as ongoing proof/direction.
8. Use only the strongest claim-matched evidence before the final action.

Long Russian or Spanish text must not be truncated by deleting maturity, evidence, or accountability meaning. Cards and regions must grow with content. A carousel must not be the only way to discover offers, work, or recent events.

## Reusable Preview Contracts

### Commercial Entry Preview

Contains:

1. offer or product identity;
2. status such as available now, paid validation, or provisional;
3. payer problem and bounded result;
4. one or two critical exclusions or fit cues when needed;
5. relevant next action.

It must not imply that provisional packaging is standardized.

### Current Work Preview

Contains:

1. system or direction identity;
2. object kind and honest current status;
3. one working capability, current result, or active question;
4. one relevant evidence or Journal cue;
5. detail action.

It does not require a purchase action.

### Event Preview

Contains:

1. time or sequence context;
2. affected system, offer, or hypothesis;
3. meaningful change, result, failure, or decision;
4. evidence strength where ambiguity would cause overclaiming;
5. event or thread action.

It must not reduce Journal to generic article thumbnails.

## Routing Breadboard

```text
Home
├── Wired Geese proposition -> How the work is made / About
├── leading commercial entry -> Offer detail / Commercial handoff
├── current work preview -> System or direction detail
├── recent event -> Event / Journal thread
├── foundation explanation -> ADSM material / teqfw.com / evidence
├── Alarisa proof -> Alarisa detail / related Journal thread
├── selected evidence -> Relevant artifact / history / About
├── final action -> Commercial handoff with context
└── footer -> All durable discovery families
```

Each affordance has one primary semantic destination. Exact links, routes, and context-passing mechanics remain later decisions.

## Evidence Responsibilities

- Every commercial preview shows a status supported by its offer document.
- Every current-work preview distinguishes working behavior from external validation.
- Recent events link chronology to an actual state change or observation.
- Alarisa evidence never implies completed engineering or economic proof.
- Older work supports only the responsibility claims it evidences.
- Home must change when authoritative status changes; it must not preserve obsolete maturity for visual stability.

## Multilingual Rules

- English, Russian, and Spanish use the same semantic regions, statuses, priorities, and actions.
- Labels and headlines may be idiomatic rather than literal.
- The Wired Geese metaphor requires culturally intelligible expression but must preserve human accountability in every locale.
- Longer localized content must not push current work, Journal, or commercial discovery behind biography or technical explanation.
- Final locale labels, metadata, and copy require separate work.

## Open Composition Decisions

- The final hero expression and visual treatment of the Wired Geese/Wild Geese metaphor.
- Which commercial entry leads Home as evidence changes.
- Which current systems and events are selected and how frequently they update.
- Whether the desktop opening pairs proposition with work, event, commercial entry, or another composition satisfying the contract.
- Final number and treatment of previews on each viewport.
- How Event/thread status, failures, corrections, and commercial evidence appear visually.
- Which claim-specific evidence belongs on Home rather than deeper pages.
- Final navigation labels, routes, imagery, components, accessibility behavior, and localized copy.

These decisions may change expression but must preserve the balance among selling, demonstrating, and documenting.

## Purpose

Define human-agent supervision of the target architecture and detect downstream drift from the current product model without freezing provisional labels, routes, offers, or systems.

## Authority

The human owns product direction, accepted commercial meaning, consequential architecture boundaries, and approval of implementation work. Agents refine documentation and implementation only inside that authority.

The normative dependency order remains:

`product -> architecture -> environment -> code`

Product status, evidence, commercial direction, and open questions must be read from `../product/`. Existing implementation and older architecture are evidence of previous state, not authority to reinstate superseded semantics.

## Human Decisions

Human approval is required for:

- product-level changes to site responsibilities, semantic hierarchy, Home duties, object status, evidence meaning, or commercial direction;
- exact final public copy and consequential route or navigation choices where product authority leaves them open;
- a new engine, runtime area, separate application, persistent state owner, external integration, or customer/private-data owner;
- website handling of credentials, private resources, payment, CRM records, provisioning, managed operation, or evidence automation;
- standardized offer scope, price, hosting, maintenance, support, or recurring model;
- source, deployment, cognitive-context, ownership, licensing, modification, operational, maintenance, and support rights for any transfer;
- retirement or redirection of public URLs when preservation value is uncertain.

## Agent Responsibilities

Agents must:

- trace product changes through architecture, environment, code guidance, authored sources, and derived output in that order;
- preserve one multilingual TeqCMS SSR/publication system and keep project-specific runtime behavior thin unless a new approved requirement contradicts it;
- implement semantic responsibilities from product authority without turning candidate labels, paths, link counts, or current prominence into permanent contracts;
- preserve direct discovery of commercial entries, current work, Journal/Events, working-model explanation, and a commercial action;
- keep Journal chronology separate from current canonical status and connect Events to affected systems, offers, hypotheses, and evidence;
- preserve honest maturity, claim-specific evidence, and the distinction between working behavior, internal use, external use, paid work, reuse, customer outcome, and recurring value;
- keep TeqFW, ADSM, Alarisa, PDE, Desks, books, offers, and evidence in their product-authorized roles;
- preserve Alex's accountability and describe AI agents as authorized participants rather than employees, owners, or independent authorities;
- distinguish ordinary outbound links and human contact from runtime integrations and authority transfers;
- keep context transfer bounded, optional, explicitly authorized, and owned by accountable humans on both sides;
- preserve valuable URLs, original historical value, explicit status, and meaningful internal discovery during later migration;
- surface missing evidence or contradictory product authority instead of inventing architecture, maturity, or commercial terms.

## Drift Signals

Architecture or lower-level drift exists when:

- Journal/Events lose direct meaningful discovery, become footer-only, or are grouped back into an equivalent secondary archive with Project Archive, Library, or Books;
- Event publication or activity volume is treated as evidence by itself, or chronology silently becomes the only owner of current product status;
- commercial paths require understanding Alarisa, PDE internals, TeqFW, or ADSM before buyer value is clear;
- Alarisa is placed above TeqFW/ADSM as the foundation, represented as completed proof or market-ready, or made the container for every system;
- TeqFW is treated as an ordinary wiredgeese.com catalogue product or wiredgeese.com begins replacing `teqfw.com` as its primary technical site;
- ADSM is reduced to internal automation or exaggerated into autonomous, developer-free maintenance;
- Telegram is presented as mature, validated, or the permanent main commercial offer;
- provisional MCP work is presented as validated, standardized, fixed-scope, or permanent site identity without product evidence;
- PDE, Telegram Desk, Shared Files Desk, or another early capability is represented as mature SaaS or a product merely because it works;
- the site reverts to a generic freelance-services or consultancy catalogue detached from bounded outcomes and the working foundation;
- the site reverts to a product catalogue or portfolio with no prominent current evidence stream;
- historical content, biography, durable knowledge, or search value dominates current commercial and development meaning;
- `Products / How it works / Alarisa` is reinstated as mandatory merely because labels or legacy routes exist;
- a current offer requires a generic Products catalogue, or exact routes and header-link counts are inferred without approval;
- Sell, Demonstrate, and Document are forced into visual symmetry, or one responsibility eliminates the other two;
- AI-agent participation obscures Alex's intent, acceptance, risk, or commercial accountability;
- customer or product credentials, private data, deployment, MCP execution, Telegram operation, storage, payment, CRM, provisioning, monitoring, or support silently migrate into the website runtime;
- source, deployment, context, ownership, modification, operational, or support rights are treated as equivalent grants;
- customer-confidential material or unrelated Wired Geese context is reused or transferred without authority;
- visits, clicks, contacts, internal use, close-user installations, or historical work are promoted into unsupported commercial validation;
- valuable multilingual URLs disappear, retained content becomes discoverable only through search/direct access, or old content is rewritten into disguised sales copy;
- GitHub Flows, Agent Orchestration PoC, or the discontinued `€50` experiment returns as active architecture;
- environment, code, templates, or generated output recreate superseded product semantics.

Loss of the literal labels `Products`, `How it works`, or a top-level `Alarisa` item is not drift when the product-authorized semantic responsibilities and visitor journeys remain intact.

## Review Discipline

For any downstream change, agents must verify:

1. which product authority establishes the affected meaning;
2. which architecture decision and constraint govern its realization;
3. whether new state, integration, authority, trust, or runtime ownership is introduced;
4. whether maintained locales and compact viewports preserve the same visitor jobs and boundaries;
5. whether Journal chronology, current status, evidence strength, and commercial claims remain distinct;
6. whether affected URLs and retained material receive deliberate treatment;
7. whether implementation remains subordinate to context.

Unresolved product questions remain unresolved downstream. Architecture and implementation must expose them for human decision rather than choosing by inertia.

## Transition Rule

The documentation target may temporarily coexist with legacy templates, routes, and code. Later implementation work must remove or repurpose obsolete semantics in reviewed stages, preserve valuable public access, and verify that no lower-level contract recreates a catalogue-only, portfolio-only, consultancy-only, archive-only, or single-offer model.

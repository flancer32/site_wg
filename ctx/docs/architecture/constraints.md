# Architecture Constraints

- Template Version: `20260605`
- Changed: `20260910`

## Purpose

Record non-negotiable architecture, semantic, evidence, trust, preservation, and approval constraints for wiredgeese.com.

## Core System Constraints

The architecture must preserve:

- `ctx/` as normative knowledge above architecture, environment, code, authored content, and generated output;
- one multilingual, content-oriented TeqCMS SSR/publication site with thin justified Node.js adaptation;
- authored templates, assets, and configuration as implementation inputs and `web/` as derived output;
- one coherent site runtime across commercial, current-work, Journal, working-model, and retained-history surfaces;
- separation between wiredgeese.com and product or customer runtimes;
- Alex Gusev's accountability for intent, consequential decisions, acceptance, and commercial authority when AI agents participate;
- deliberate preservation of valuable multilingual URLs and public material.

The current product model does not itself authorize a new site engine, client application, content database, event store, analytics backend, or offer-specific runtime.

## Semantic Surface Constraints

The public architecture must:

- support `SELL`, `DEMONSTRATE`, and `DOCUMENT` as first-class responsibilities while allowing aligned selling greater current operational and visual priority;
- keep current commercial entries, current work, and Journal/Events directly discoverable without requiring traversal through one another;
- keep Journal/Events as an active evidence surface rather than grouping it with ordinary archives, Library, books, or historical projects;
- connect Events and cumulative threads to affected systems, offers, hypotheses, evidence, and current status;
- treat Journal prominence as a discovery and relationship requirement, not as a publishing quota, social-feed mandate, or activity-theatre incentive;
- keep About, books, durable knowledge, projects, and history intentionally reachable without allowing them to control first-time attention;
- keep a distinct commercial action available without assuming automated sales behavior;
- allow Home to realize the product-authorized attention order on wide and compact viewports.

Architecture must not freeze exact labels, routes, header-link counts, visual symmetry, permanent prominence of a current offer, or permanent prominence of a current system where product authority leaves them open.

In particular, it must not restore `Products / How it works / Alarisa` as mandatory permanent primary semantics. A generic Products catalogue must not be a prerequisite for reaching an offer. Journal must not be footer-only. Alarisa need not be a permanent header item when current-work discovery exposes it properly.

## Product-Meaning Constraints

Architecture must preserve these product-authorized distinctions without becoming their independent status authority:

- Wired Geese is the public model and brand of one accountable human maker plus hired AI agents, not software, a platform, a fictional organization, or generic freelance positioning.
- TeqFW is the technical North Star and application platform; primary detailed promotion belongs to `teqfw.com`.
- ADSM is the complementary development-management methodology, not a promise of autonomous or developer-free evolution.
- Alarisa is the major ongoing proof system and long-term product direction, not the foundation, completed proof, universal container, or market-ready purchase object.
- PDE and Desks are working but early external systems or capabilities, not mature SaaS or automatic catalogue products.
- MCP integration may be a primary provisional near-term commercial entry without becoming permanent site identity, standardized delivery, or website runtime responsibility.
- Telegram is paid early access and validation, not the assumed primary mature offer or permanent cash engine.
- Books are available knowledge products whose purchase paths and evidence meaning differ from software and engineering delivery.

No current system, offer, or strategic emphasis may be promoted beyond the status in `../product/product-system.md` and its applicable offer authority.

## Anti-Reduction Constraints

The architecture must prevent wiredgeese.com from collapsing into:

- a catalogue in which every system or capability must become a product;
- a portfolio with working examples but no actionable commercial path;
- a generic consultancy or service menu detached from the owned technical direction;
- an archive or blog in which historical content or publication volume replaces current work and commercial meaning;
- a single-offer funnel in which MCP, Telegram, Alarisa, or another provisional object becomes permanent site identity;
- a TeqFW documentation or marketing replacement for `teqfw.com`.

## Evidence And State Constraints

The architecture must keep Event chronology distinct from current canonical product or system state.

It must not treat visits, clicks, page events, contact attempts, Journal posts, commits, agent activity, internal use, or publication volume as automatic proof of payment, delivery, customer outcome, repeatability, market validation, or recurring value.

Journal may expose evidence but does not manufacture it. Product facts and status changes must remain governed by product authority and claim-specific evidence. No database, evidence-management application, CRM, or analytics system may be invented merely to satisfy the Journal model.

## Runtime And Trust Constraints

The website must not automatically become or own:

- a PDE runtime or Desk host;
- a customer MCP server or agent execution environment;
- Telegram credentials, sessions, messages, channels, or publishing authority;
- Shared Files or other customer storage;
- customer service credentials, private resources, operational logs, or deployment state;
- payment processing, provisioning, CRM records, managed hosting, monitoring, support, or incident response;
- an automated or synchronized cognitive-context distribution service.

Access to a capability does not grant authority to use customer resources. Credential ownership, administrative access, configuration, retention, updates, revocation, evidence disclosure, and operational responsibility must remain explicit in each external agreement.

The architecture must not promise absolute security, privacy, availability, compliance, scale, enterprise capacity, unlimited customization, `24/7` support, or continuity independent of Alex.

Source access, deployment access, cognitive-context access, ownership, licensing, modification rights, operational authority, maintenance, warranties, and support remain separate grants. Context transfer must be bounded, human-reviewed, and free of unrelated or unauthorized material.

## Commercial Constraints

The site may support manual qualification, scoping, agreement, payment, and delivery while commercial models are being validated. Architecture must not invent fixed price, schedule, SLA, supported-service matrix, standardized delivery, subscription, or recurring-value claims.

Customer-funded work may inform priorities and produce reusable learning when rights permit. It must not redefine TeqFW's North Star, turn customer-specific requirements into foundation policy automatically, expose confidential material, or weaken the agreed customer outcome.

GitHub Flows, Agent Orchestration PoC, and the discontinued `€50` experiment remain historical, not active offer or validation architecture.

## Preservation And Multilingual Constraints

Route or content retirement requires an evidence-informed keep, repurpose, redirect, archive-access, or reviewed-removal decision. Retained material must have a meaningful internal discovery path; server presence or search access alone is insufficient.

Preservation does not imply primary prominence or current endorsement. Older content must keep its original value and truthful status rather than being rewritten into disguised sales copy.

English, Russian, and Spanish surfaces must preserve the same identity, object status, evidence boundaries, visitor jobs, and commercial actions. Idiomatic labels may differ. Responsive or localized composition must not delete maturity, accountability, trust, current-work, Journal, or commercial meaning.

## Approval Cases

Human approval is required before introducing:

- a new site engine, runtime area, major architectural owner, separate application, or persistent source of truth;
- a new external runtime integration or customer/private-data owner;
- website handling of credentials, private resources, payment, provisioning, managed operation, or CRM records;
- analytics or evidence automation that assigns commercial meaning;
- a synchronized or continuing context-transfer service;
- a semantic hierarchy or Home responsibility that contradicts product authority;
- retirement or redirection of public URLs when preservation value is uncertain.

## Purpose

Define page-to-page navigation and discovery relationships for the target multilingual SSR surface without selecting unresolved target paths or redefining product-level information architecture.

## Primary Navigation

The shared header exposes these meanings in this order:

1. Wired Geese brand -> locale Home;
2. Products -> Products catalogue;
3. How it works -> cross-product production, control, customization, and continuation explanation;
4. Alarisa -> vision and exploration destination;
5. distinct commercial action -> shared commercial handoff;
6. locale control -> semantically equivalent destination in another maintained locale.

Home is not duplicated as a text item when the brand link is evident. About, Project Archive, Library, Journal, Books, individual technologies, and individual products remain outside permanent primary navigation.

## Home Navigation

Home follows the semantic connections defined in `../../../product/home-page-composition.md`:

- product-maker proposition -> Products catalogue and How it works;
- current product preview -> relevant Product Detail;
- product-specific action -> shared commercial handoff with product context when justified;
- adaptation bridge -> shared commercial handoff or How it works;
- How-it-works preview -> How it works;
- Alarisa preview -> Alarisa;
- accountable-maker summary -> About;
- selected proof -> the directly relevant proof destination;
- final action -> shared commercial handoff;
- footer group -> selected primary, secondary knowledge, maker, or contact destination.

The first product remains a direct navigation object on desktop and mobile. Responsive composition must not hide it behind biography, method, vision, or archive discovery.

## Product And Trust Navigation

- Products routes to every catalogue-eligible Product Detail and exposes the shared adaptation possibility.
- Product Detail routes to the commercial handoff, related products, relevant proof, and How it works when control, customization, or production depth helps the decision.
- How it works routes back to Products and relevant Product Details, to About or inspectable evidence for trust depth, to Alarisa for vision, and to the commercial handoff for a qualified need.
- Alarisa routes to Products only when an independent product genuinely emerged from that direction and to How it works when production foundations help understanding. Alarisa has no purchase action of its own.
- About routes to Products, How it works, selected proof, and the commercial handoff without becoming a primary acquisition category.

## Secondary Discovery

The footer provides the accepted Discover, Knowledge, and Wired Geese semantic groups. Project Archive, Library, Journal, Books, historical pages, and technical material also connect contextually to current Products, How it works, Alarisa, or About only when the relationship is genuine.

Retained content must have at least one intentional internal discovery path. Search or a surviving URL alone is insufficient. The footer links to collection-level destinations and must not become a duplicate product catalogue or unfiltered archive.

## Locale Navigation

Locale switching preserves the equivalent destination, query, and fragment when browser enhancement is available and falls back to the target locale root otherwise. Required commercial page families remain semantically equivalent across English, Russian, and Spanish.

Final localized labels are copy decisions. Navigation meaning, order, and primary-versus-secondary status are invariant.

## Exceptional Outcomes

- A retained legacy path follows its approved keep, repurpose, redirect, archive-access, or retirement treatment.
- A redirect preserves locale and query information where the approved mapping requires it.
- An unresolved HTML destination reaches the localized not-found surface.
- A discontinued offer does not remain in primary or product navigation merely because its route survives.
- A missing target-locale equivalent must not silently send the visitor to a semantically different page.

Exact routes, redirect mappings, context-passing mechanisms, and post-contact behavior remain migration and implementation decisions.

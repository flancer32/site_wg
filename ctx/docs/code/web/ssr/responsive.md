## Purpose

Define viewport-dependent composition and attention constraints for the multilingual SSR surface.

## Shared Reflow

Wide layouts may use multiple columns; medium layouts reduce density; compact layouts use a keyboard-operable menu and readable single-column collections; all content remains usable at `320px` CSS width without horizontal page scrolling. Reflow may change emphasis and repetition but must not hide substantive content, maturity information, required actions, or destination identity.

## Home Attention

Before significant scrolling, a visitor must be able to comprehend Wired Geese and find an early commercially actionable path. Current work and meaningful Journal discovery must remain visible early enough to serve Demonstrate and Document. Deep methodology, biography, durable/history material, and Alarisa must not displace those responsibilities; Alarisa must not accidentally dominate the opening.

On compact screens, `SELL` may receive stronger initial attention, but current work and Journal discovery must remain direct. Do not hard-code a first product, card count, carousel, hero arrangement, or a rule that Products precede Journal. Collections grow with translated content and never clip meaning through fixed heights.

## Accessibility And Enhancement

Reading and focus order match visible order. Actions remain distinguishable without color alone. Touch targets, focus, reduced motion, semantic headings, and alternative text follow `layouts.md`. Browser JavaScript may enhance menu, locale, and link behavior; core content and navigation remain available in SSR output. Exact breakpoints and geometry remain design decisions.

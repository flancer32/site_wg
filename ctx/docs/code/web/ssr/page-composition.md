## Purpose

Define implementation-facing composition constraints that realize the accepted Architecture while leaving copy, visual design, and target route selection open.

## Stable Composition

- Locale `inc/layout.html` remains the common outer shell; `inc/nav.html` and `inc/footer.html` remain shared.
- Page templates provide route metadata and content inside that shell.
- Existing Journal indexes receive prepared item collections through render enrichment; authored content and TeqCMS remain the publication model.
- Shared accessibility, localization, canonical, alternate-link, and responsive rules apply to all page families.

## Target Composition

Composition must be able to support `SELL + DEMONSTRATE + DOCUMENT`, with aligned commercial access receiving current operational attention without converting Journal into archive material. It implements the page-family responsibilities in `../../../../architecture/structure.md` rather than copying product strategy.

Home must support the following product-authorized dependency order: Wired Geese proposition and accountable maker; early current commercial path; current work; recent meaningful Journal evidence; human-plus-agent model; TeqFW/ADSM foundation; Alarisa as major ongoing proof; selected evidence and maker trust; commercial next action; and deeper durable discovery. Adjacent meanings may share a visual section. Exact copy, card counts, layout geometry, and section order within compatible early attention are open.

Journal/Event chronology is distinct from current canonical system or offer state. Current status is communicated by status-bearing destinations and may link to supporting Events. Alarisa is not a required primary destination or the foundation above TeqFW/ADSM. Books, Library, projects, and history remain durable discovery separate from active evidence.

## Current State And Design Gate

Current Home templates lead with the provisional MCP commercial path, retain Telegram paid validation and books, then show a bounded Current Work preview and recent Journal projection. `/projects.html` gives active current systems their own status cards before a historical archive. These are current implementation facts, not permanent ordering or labels. Do not add a product runtime, database, event service, forms, analytics, payments, provisioning, or a new template system without approved architecture and interface decisions.

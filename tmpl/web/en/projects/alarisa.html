Long-term direction · Active exploration

Alarisa

A vision for the relationship between a Principal—the person an Assistant serves—and their personal Assistant in the digital world.

Alarisa is my long-term development direction and a major ongoing multi-component system. I am exploring it through practical work today; over time it may take the form of one product or a connected family of products. Its final form is not fixed, and Alarisa itself is not currently a commercial product.

A Principal and an Assistant

The Principal is the person the Assistant serves. The Principal owns the intent, remains the human authority, and lives with the consequences of important decisions.

PrincipalSets intent, keeps authority, and decides what matters.
AssistantBuilds useful context, helps understand situations, and supports action.
Defined boundariesDigital resources and consequential actions remain visible and controlled.

What this direction explores

Alarisa asks how an Assistant can retain useful context and continuity, work with digital services within defined boundaries, support delegation, and interact with other people, their services, and their agents—without dissolving human responsibility.

Foundation and current proof

TeqFW is the technical foundation and ADSM is the development-management methodology behind this ongoing proof. They enable the work; Alarisa does not replace them as the foundation.

The proof is experimental and incomplete. Current systems such as PDE may contribute useful working evidence without making Alarisa market-ready.

The Alarisa repository is an inspectable entry to the system's source estate. Its related components are evidence of real multi-component development, not seven separate products or a claim of market readiness.

From exploration to useful products

A direction can lead to experiments, capabilities, and technical systems. Some become independently useful products; some remain infrastructure; some are abandoned when they stop proving useful. This is exploration, not a predetermined product roadmap.

PDE is one technical system that emerged while exploring how agents can access controlled digital resources. It has value beyond any complete Alarisa product.

ChatGPT + Telegram is a paid early-access validation offer using PDE-related infrastructure. It is useful on its own: a visitor does not need to adopt the Alarisa vision to consider it.

A stable direction, not a fixed roadmap

Alarisa may eventually become a product or product family, but no final architecture or catalogue is promised today. Independent products can stand on their own along the way, while experiments that do not help can be left behind.

Current status

Alarisa is an active long-term exploration, not a product available to buy or try. See current projects and systems, recent Journal evidence, or how I work with AI agents.

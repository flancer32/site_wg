# NutriLog Open Beta Launched

I'm pleased to announce the launch of [NutriLog](https://nutrilog.app.wiredgeese.com/) — a personal AI assistant for
        calorie tracking, nutrition analysis, and weight goal management.

Starting April 1st, we've launched an **open beta**: the first **100 users** get
        **3 months of free subscription**. To attract this audience, I ran a small experiment with
        **Google Ads** — directing limited but precisely targeted traffic.

NutriLog is built on [TeqFW](https://github.com/teqfw/di) —
        a lightweight modular JavaScript platform I developed specifically for reliable architecture and managed
        development.

The system combines a GPT-like chat with external memory: users maintain conversational freedom while gaining
        structure — goals, progress tracking, and visualization. If you're interested in a self-directed approach to
        nutrition and weight control — try NutriLog. Your feedback is most welcome!

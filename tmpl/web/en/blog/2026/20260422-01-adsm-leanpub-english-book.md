# My ADSM book in English is on Leanpub

<zoom-img src="/img/blog/2026/20260422-01-adsm-leanpub-english-book.jpg"
        alt="My ADSM book in English is on Leanpub" width="100px"></zoom-img>

The [English version](https://fly.wiredgeese.com/flancer/leanpub/adsm-en/) of my ADSM
        book is now on Leanpub. I translated it with a Codex agent, and it took about 10% of my weekly Codex quota
        on GPT Plus.

What made the translation straightforward was that I kept the original Russian manuscript in Markdown. That
        was the key factor, and the book is short, about 60 pages, so I could get through it quickly.

This follows the [previous note](/en/blog/2026/20260421-01-adsm-leanpub-russian-book.html) about
        the Russian edition on Leanpub. The result feels solid enough, although I would not call myself a strong
        English writer. If any subscribers want a free copy, they can ask for it through my Telegram channels:
        [ru](https://t.me/alexgusev_lab_ru),
        [en](https://t.me/alexgusev_lab_en),
        [es](https://t.me/alexgusev_lab_es).

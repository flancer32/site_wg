# TeqFW Guide: DTO — a personal architecture assistant

<zoom-img src="/img/brand/teqfw.webp" alt="TeqFW Guide: DTO — a personal architecture assistant"
        width="100px"></zoom-img>

I launched [TeqFW Guide: DTO](https://fly.wiredgeese.com/flancer/gpt/teqfw/guide/dto/) — a personal GPT assistant for working with TeqFW architecture. In this framework, a DTO has a
        strictly defined place and function, which differs from common interpretations. Developers join with their own
        experience and mental models, and for a team it matters to align those models around a single architectural
        baseline. The assistant is grounded in a fixed corpus of documents and system rules, provides stable
        interpretation, and conveys a coherent view of DTOs in TeqFW. It adapts the dialogue to the user’s level and
        helps align understanding without sacrificing architectural integrity. It’s a tool for onboarding and day-to-day
        work that helps keep a single architectural voice across the project.

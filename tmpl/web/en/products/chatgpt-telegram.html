Control and trust

This is more than a conventional Telegram bot.

The integration can work through your Telegram account rather than only a Bot API identity. That can make access broader than a bot’s, but it also makes the deployment and access boundary important.

Telegram account access involves sensitive credentials and session data. Before setup, we make the boundary visible: where the software runs, where configuration and session data are stored, who controls the host, who has administrative access, who handles updates, and how access can later be removed or revoked.

Depending on the situation, this may be your existing VPS, a dedicated VPS you control, another dedicated host, or Alex-managed hosting for a demo, test, or experimental use. The arrangement is discussed explicitly before setup.

Fit and boundaries

Useful for an early adopter with a concrete need.

This is a good fit if you already use ChatGPT in real work, have Telegram information or publishing work worth improving, and are comfortable agreeing a clear deployment and trust boundary with the person building it.

It is working software Alex uses himself, with selected capabilities installed for a small number of close or experimental users. It is not a mature mass-market SaaS, a promise of every Telegram operation, or an open-ended automation service. Each setup is limited to the workflows we agree.

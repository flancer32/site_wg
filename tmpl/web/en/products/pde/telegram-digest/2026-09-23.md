# «Вастрик.Ай» digest for the previous 24 hours

Anthropic cut Opus 5.5 API prices to $4 per million input tokens and $20 per million output tokens. Cache reads now cost $0.20 per million tokens, writes $5. Claims that Opus matches Astra at a fraction of the cost met some skepticism: people questioned whether benchmark token savings carry over to everyday work. Hands-on reports varied. One user said Opus spent longer investigating a task and surfaced edge cases; others liked its writing but found it expensive to run. In one example, Opus Max with ten subagents used 12% of a $100 plan’s weekly quota in 90 minutes.

GPT-6 Sol and Luna also launched. In the published BitGN evaluation, Sol high scored 100% on the AI coding set; its authors called it fast and cost-effective. Some users, though, saw Sol 6 as a regression from 5.6: it can be verbose and overengineer short tasks. Luna was often recommended for simpler work, Sol for day-to-day tasks, and Astra for harder analysis, but no consensus ranking emerged. The new banked resets prompted questions about whether they shift the regular reset date; that remained unclear.

One useful project was [agent-room](https://github.com/neonick/agent-room), a local chat where Codex, Claude, and Grok can respond to and challenge one another. For app automation, participants recommended checking for an API, CLI, or AppleScript interface first, then falling back to mouse-driven computer use when needed. OpenAI also announced an improvement to GPT-6 prompt caching: https://openai.com/index/better-prompt-caching-for-gpt-6/

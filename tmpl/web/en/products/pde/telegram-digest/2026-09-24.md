# «Вастрик.Ай» digest for the previous 24 hours

Sol 6 reviews were split. Some users found it verbose and prone to overengineering even short tasks, and a few went back to Sol 5.6; others liked its clearer answers. Opus 5.5 also got praise, though cost and quotas remain part of the decision. One participant reported spending 12% of a $100 plan’s weekly allowance in 90 minutes with Opus Max and ten subagents. In their experience, the usual two or three parallel agents use considerably less.

The banked-reset discussion showed why shifting a weekly reset date works for some people who burn through their quota early and frustrates others who budget usage through the workweek. Several wanted a user-controlled choice. Resets are still irregular, making them hard to plan around.

For automation, participants recommended checking for an app’s CLI/API or AppleScript actions before resorting to mouse-driven computer use. Separately, one participant outlined a cyber-risk scenario: if AI agents make vulnerability discovery and exploitation much cheaper, adaptive attacks could become easier to produce. That was a forecast, not an established outcome.

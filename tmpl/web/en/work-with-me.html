How we start

Begin with a bounded first piece of the real product.

  1. Describe the product needWe establish who it serves, what it should enable, and what is known now.
  2. Make constraints explicitWe identify integrations, operational requirements, compliance, deployment, and other hard conditions.
  3. Choose the first scopeWe agree a useful initial deliverable and how its result will be recognized.
  4. Agree scope and pricePrice follows the agreed scope; there is no public hourly rate or invented package price.
  5. Build and decide what followsA successful first build can become the next agreed scope or longer-term product engineering.

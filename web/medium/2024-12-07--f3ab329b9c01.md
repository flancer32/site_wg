# Thank You and Farewell, Medium!

Dear friends, readers, and fellow enthusiasts,

I started writing here to share my thoughts on software development and connect with like-minded people. I hoped to spark discussions and get valuable feedback.

However, maintaining a paid subscription has always been a requirement for publishing here. In the past, I believed it was worth it, but now I find myself questioning that value. Perhaps the topics I care deeply about aren’t as engaging for others here, and that’s okay.

To each of my 84 subscribers, I want to say a heartfelt thank you. If you’ve found value in my posts, your interest means the world to me.

As for what’s next — while I’m leaving Medium, I’m not done writing. I’m not sure what format it will take, but writing code and sharing my thoughts with others is something I can’t help but do.

If you’d like to stay connected, you can find me on [LinkedIn](https://www.linkedin.com/in/aleksandrs-gusevs-011ba928/). I might also create my own site at [flancer32.com](https://flancer32.com/) or [wiredgeese.com](https://wiredgeese.com/) — but no promises just yet!

Sometimes, there are things you do simply because you must. These things are what make life truly meaningful. I’ll keep writing, coding, and seeking ways to connect with others. Just not here, not on Medium.

Thank you again for being part of this journey.
